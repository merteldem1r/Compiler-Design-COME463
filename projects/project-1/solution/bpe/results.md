## 3. Byte-level fallback (why BPE never reports an error)

- `@` (1 UTF-8 byte) → 1 BPE token: `@`
- `ğ` (2 UTF-8 bytes) → 1 BPE token: `ğ`
- `𓂀` (4 UTF-8 bytes) → 4 BPE tokens: `\xF0` · `\x93` · `\x82` · `\x80`
